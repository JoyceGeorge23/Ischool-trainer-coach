=== SLIDE 43 ===
Quality
Quality management in our Sessions is the act of
overseeing different activities and tasks within the
session to ensure that session quality and level of
education offered, as well as the means used to provide
them, are consistent. It helps to achieve and maintain a
desired level of quality within the education department
in ISchool.


=== SLIDE 44 ===
The iSchool Quality Team evaluates a random
sample of the instructor sessions each month,
according to the the iSchool Quality Guidelines
and Evaluation Criteria, the number of sessions
reviewed is proportional to the number of
sessions each instructor gives in a given month.
