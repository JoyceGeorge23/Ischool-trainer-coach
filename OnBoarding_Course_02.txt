Presentation
Mastery of presentation skills enhances professional reputation and fosters meaningful
academic interactions. Therefore, developing these skills is essential for successful teaching 
experiences and for promoting academic growth.

=== SLIDE 5 ===
The tone of voice:
Your voice is the primary means of communication between the
teacher and
the student, especially in distance education. Therefore, the
teacher’s voice
must be clear and strong, avoiding any impression of sleepiness
or boredom.
It should not be too loud, as that might convey tension or anxiety. 
Presentation skills

=== SLIDE 6 ===
Confidence:
Project confidence through posture, gestures, and vocal delivery.
Believe in the content you're presenting and convey enthusiasm
for the topic. Manage nerves using relaxation techniques and
positive self-talk.
Presentation skills

=== SLIDE 7 ===
Preparation:
Research the topic thoroughly to gain a
comprehensive understanding. Organize the
content logically to ensure a smooth flow.
Presentation skills

=== SLIDE 8 ===
Adaptability:
Stay flexible with timing and content delivery
to accommodate any changes or questions.
Demonstrate adaptability and resilience in
handling technical issues or disruptions.
Presentation skills

=== SLIDE 9 ===
Clarity:
Use simple language and avoid jargon to 
ensure comprehension by all audience members.
Structure the presentation with a clear introduction,
body, and conclusion..
Presentation skills

=== SLIDE 10 ===
Communication
Communication skills are vital for iSchool instructors, as
they enable effective interaction with students. These
skills help convey information, adapt to diverse learning
needs, and foster a positive learning environment.

=== SLIDE 11 ===
Communication skills
Ice breaking:
We need to break the ice with the student to get to know them better.
Tyeps:
Question-Based Icebreakers (Favorites, Fun facts, Goals and
ambitions)
Game-Based Icebreakers(eg: Kahoot quizzes, two truths and a
lie)
Creative Icebreakers (eg: Draw yourself)
Funny / Relaxed Icebreakers (eg: Funny introductions, Meme
sharing)
Digital / Online Icebreakers (eg: polls, or wheels)
Reflection Icebreakers (eg: One goal for today, Proud moments)

=== SLIDE 12 ===
Engagement:
Use interactive elements such as questions, polls, or discussions
to engage the audience. Maintain eye contact and body language
that convey confidence and openness. Adapt to the audience's
level of understanding and interests to keep them engaged.
Communication skills

=== SLIDE 13 ===
Patience and Understanding:
Exercise patience and understanding, especially
with beginner students. Listen openly to their
questions, respond positively, and adjust
explanations to match their cognitive level.
Communication skills

=== SLIDE 14 ===
Friendly and Encouraging Language:
Use friendly and encouraging language when
communicating with children. Employ supportive
words to foster their progress and boost their
confidence..
Communication skills

=== SLIDE 15 ===
Constructive feedback:
Your feedback should be constructive and descriptive.
Communication skills
Examples:
“Your logic is correct, which is great. Now focus on making the
code cleaner and easier to read.”
“Good attempt at solving the problem. Try debugging step by
step to find errors more efficiently.”
“You completed the task well. Managing your time a little better
could help you finish earlier.”
“You explained your idea clearly. Try organizing the points a bit
more to improve readability.”
“I like how engaged you were today. Next time, try giving more
detailed answers.”

=== SLIDE 16 ===
Management 
Management skills can be defined as certain attributes or abilities that an executive
 should possess in order to fulfill specific tasks in an organization. They include the 
capacity to perform executive duties in an organization while avoiding crisis situations 
and promptly solving problems when they occur.

=== SLIDE 17 ===
Management skills
Time Management:
Divide the session into manageable parts to ensure it stays
within the allotted time frame. Explain each segment clearly,
ensuring that students grasp the material effectively.

=== SLIDE 18 ===
Learn and Make:
Encourage students to apply their knowledge during the session through
hands-on activities. This approach solidifies understanding and motivates
continued learning by showcasing tangible outcomes..
Management skills

=== SLIDE 19 ===
Planning:
The planning process includes identifying and setting achievable
goals, developing necessary strategies, and outlining the tasks and
schedules on how to achieve the set goals. Without a good plan,
little can be achieved.
Management skills

=== SLIDE 20 ===
Problem-solving:
Problem-solving is another essential skill. A good tutor must have the
ability to tackle and solve the frequent problems that can arise in a
typical workday. Problem-solving in management involves identifying a
certain problem or situation and then finding the best way to handle the
problem and get the best solution.
Management skills
