Quality
Quality management in our Sessions is the act of
overseeing different activities and tasks within the
session to ensure that session quality and level of
education offered, as well as the means used to provide
them, are consistent. It helps to achieve and maintain a
desired level of quality within the education department
in ISchool.

=== SLIDE 12 ===
The iSchool Quality Team evaluates a random
sample of the instructor sessions each month,
according to the the iSchool Quality Guidelines
and Evaluation Criteria, the number of sessions
reviewed is proportional to the number of
sessions each instructor gives in a given month.
Quality Process


=== SLIDE 13 ===
Instructors are rated in 5 main categories each one of
which has its subcategories, each category has its
weight in the final evaluation report which is provided
to the instructors at the end of each month, along with
the feedback of any exceptional performance or any
guideline breach ( Red, Yellow Flags).
Quality Process

=== SLIDE 17 ===
Flags Policy


=== SLIDE 18 ===
Yellow flags are raised in instances where a mistake or breach of
guidelines occurs for the first time during a session. These flags serve
as constructive feedback and reminders to adhere closely to the
established guidelines. They signify areas for improvement and offer an
opportunity for self-reflection and corrective action.
Yellow Flag


=== SLIDE 19 ===
It is the violation of any Rules or Guidelines that might affect the
quality of the session.
The Yellow flag is not deductible from the salary but it will affect The
monthly quality score which will affect the rank upgrade and the
incentives.
The First violation will result in a yellow Flag.
If the violation is repeated Within the month / Next Month, it will be
considered as a Red Flag.
Yellow Flag


=== SLIDE 20 ===
Red flags are indicative of repeated mistakes or significant breaches of
the established guidelines. When a situation warrants a red flag, it
underscores the need for immediate attention and corrective
measures. It signifies a critical deviation from the expected standards
and emphasizes the importance of rectifying the issue promptly to
maintain session quality, and it’s deductible from the tutor’s monthly
salary.
Red Flag


=== SLIDE 21 ===
It is the violation of a crucial rule or a guideline which affects the
session quality.
Repeating a yellow flag is considered a red flag.
The Red flag is deductible from the salary and will affect the
monthly quality score and the rank upgrade and the incentives.
Red Flag


=== SLIDE 22 ===
Green flags represent exceptional effort and outstanding work
exhibited during a session. These flags are raised to acknowledge and
celebrate instances of exemplary performance that go above and
beyond the norm. They serve as positive reinforcement, motivating
team members to continue delivering exceptional sessions and
contributing to the overall success of our program.
Green Flag


=== SLIDE 23 ===
Green flags are raised to exceptional effort
during the sessions an are rewardable


=== SLIDE 24 ===
The salary deduction will be as follows:
1 Red flag: 3% salary deduction.
2 Red flags: 5% salary deduction.
3 Red flags: 7% salary deduction.
4 or more Red flags will result in the instructor’s Re-evaluation.
If any Red Flag is repeated Within the month / Next Month for the same
reason, it will be calculated as twice the number of flags.
