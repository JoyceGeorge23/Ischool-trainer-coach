=== SLIDE 23 ===
iSchool Free Sessions
Free Sessions is a module designed for
students who haven't subscribed to
iSchool yet. It consists of 6 sessions.
This module is intended to introduce
kids to our courses before they apply.


=== SLIDE 24 ===
iSchool Free Sessions
Purpose of the Free Sessions:
Engage young minds and spark curiosity about the
course content and activities.
Provide a sneak peek into the exciting topics,
projects, and skills covered in the course.
Introduce the instructors and showcase their passion
for teaching and guiding students.
Offer hands-on activities to let students experience
the fun and educational aspects of the course.

SESSION STRUCTURE 
Our sessions at iSchool are one-to-one, lasting one hour and divided into three main parts
Learn
Make
Share


=== SLIDE 28 ===
SESSION STRUCTURE 
Our sessions at iSchool are group sessions, lasting one hour and divided into three main parts
Learn
Make
Share


=== SLIDE 29 ===
SESSION STRUCTURE 
Learn:
We explain the key concepts of the sessions
using slides and then apply them through the
main project.
Make:
We ask the student to share their screen and
work on the project on their side
Share:
Finally, we have the students share what they
learned during the sessions

=== SLIDE 31 ===
STUDY
Ensure you're familiar with the topic (slides).
Try the project on the software before the live session.
Watch the attached videos in the slides to understand the content and learn how to explain it effectively.
The manual (Training Guide) is there to help you manage session time and
       guide you in executing the project or explaining concepts.
The video is a sample demonstrating how sessions are explained with 
       examples and how the instructor applied the project.
