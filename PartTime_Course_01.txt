What Is the Assessment ?
At the end of each module, you are required to submit a video
explaining the Make activity.
While recording:
Share your screen and demonstrate your work using the
software covered in the module.
Keep your camera on throughout the recording.
Clearly explain what you are doing and how you completed
the task.
Upload your video through the assessment form before the
submission deadline.


=== SLIDE 20 ===

  1- Share your screen while recording.
  2- Keep your camera and microphone on, ensuring both are of good quality.

Learn:
We explain the key concepts of the sessions
using slides and then apply them through the
main project.

work on the project on their side
Share:
Finally, we have the students share what they
learned during the sessions

=== SLIDE 49 ===
HOW TO JOIN A SESSION 
Your upcoming session is displayed in the top bar, where you can see how much time
is left before it starts.
The “Join Now” button becomes available 10 minutes before the session.
By clicking “Join Now,” you will be automatically directed to your session.
You can use the “Ping Parent” button to notify parents about the session time.
The Materials button allows you to easily access all the resources needed for the
session.


=== SLIDE 50 ===
02
PROJECTS 
Projects are practical tasks completed by students to apply what they have learned during the sessions.
Tutors are responsible for reviewing
each project, providing clear feedback,
and marking it within 24 hours (one
working day) of submission.
Any project that is not reviewed within
24 hours (one working day) will be
marked as late.
Note: only working days are counted in
the 24-hour review period. Weekly days
off are not included and will not be
considered late.
